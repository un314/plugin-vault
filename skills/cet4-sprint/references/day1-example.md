## 2) 听力跟读（文字版，可手机朗读听一遍）
**A:** Hey, have you started preparing for CET-4? / 嘿，你开始准备四级了吗？
**B:** Not really. I keep procrastinating because I'm busy with assignments. / 还没。我一直在拖延，因为作业很多。
**A:** The exam is December 12th — only about seven weeks left. / 考试在12月12号，只剩大约七周了。
**B:** My biggest problem is listening. I struggle to catch words at natural speed. / 我最大的问题是听力，语速正常时我跟不上词。
**A:** I made a schedule: 90 minutes of study nightly, plus 20 minutes of listening. / 我定了计划：每晚学90分钟，再加20分钟听力。
**B:** That sounds efficient. Let's review vocabulary together on weekends. / 听起来很高效。周末我们一起复习词汇吧。

**自测问题：** ①考试日期？ ②B 最大的困难？ ③A 的 schedule 是什么？

## 3) 阅读精练
**The Power of Small Daily Habits（微小日常习惯的力量）**
Many students believe learning English requires long, tiring study sessions. / 很多学生认为学英语需要漫长而累人的学习时段。
However, research shows short, daily practice is more effective than occasional cramming. / 然而研究表明，短而每日的练习比偶尔的突击更有效。
For example, reading an English article for 20 minutes each day builds vocabulary steadily. / 例如，每天读20分钟英文文章能稳步积累词汇。
Listening to a short dialogue while commuting also helps the ear get used to natural speed. / 通勤时听一小段对话也能让耳朵习惯正常语速。
The key is consistency, not intensity. / 关键在于坚持，而不是强度。
A student who reviews ten words every morning will remember more than one who memorizes a hundred words the night before a test. / 每天早晨复习十个词的学生，会比考前一晚背一百个词的学生记得更牢。

**题目：**
1. 比 cramming（突击）更有效的是什么？ → 短而每日的练习
2. 文中举了什么例子说明积累词汇？ → 每天读20分钟英文文章
3. 结尾的"key"指什么？ → 坚持（consistency），而非强度（intensity）
